## Cumulative quiz (10 questions, spanning Days 1–6)
1. Order the boot stages: firmware, GRUB, kernel init, initramfs, PID 1 exec.
2. What does `journalctl -b -1` show, and what must be enabled for it to work?
3. What's the difference between `>`, `>>`, and `2>&1`, and why does order matter in
   `2>&1 > file` vs `> file 2>&1`?
4. What's stored in `/etc/shadow` that isn't in `/etc/passwd`, and why is it root-only?
5. Why must `usermod -G` be used with `-a`, and what happens if you forget it?
6. Convert `rwxr-x---` to octal, and explain what SGID does on a directory.
7. What's the difference between an ACL grant (`setfacl`) and standard rwx?
8. BRE vs ERE — which regex metacharacters need `-E` to work unescaped?
9. Why does `uniq -c` need pre-sorted input, and what's the "most common first" idiom?
10. In awk, what do `NF`, `NR`, and `$0` mean, and how do you set a custom field
    separator?
