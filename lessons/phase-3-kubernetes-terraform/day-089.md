## Theory (1h) — how to run and take a mock interview well
- Structure: 30 min architecture/conceptual Q&A (instructor asks, the learner
  answers out loud, no notes), 45 min live debugging scenario (instructor
  breaks something on a kind cluster, the learner diagnoses and fixes it
  while narrating), 15 min feedback + gap list.
- What "good" sounds like architecturally: not reciting definitions, but
  explaining WHY a design choice exists and what breaks without it (e.g.,
  not just "etcd stores state" but "if etcd is unreachable, the API
  server can't accept writes, so nothing new can be scheduled even though
  existing pods keep running — the blast radius is bounded but real").
- What "good" sounds like in live debugging: narrate the funnel from Day
  73 out loud (status → events → logs → exec → dependency) BEFORE typing
  — interviewers weight process as much as the final fix; silent
  fumbling reads worse than a slower, narrated, methodical path to the
  same answer.
- Gap list discipline: every question the learner can't answer cleanly
  gets written down verbatim during the session (not paraphrased after)
  — this becomes the literal study list for the days before Day 90's
  capstone.

## Architecture Q&A bank (instructor asks these, or generates variants; ~30 min)
1. Walk me through what happens from `kubectl apply -f deployment.yaml`
   to a running container, naming every component involved.
2. Why does a Deployment need a ReplicaSet in between it and the Pods —
   why not manage Pods directly?
3. A Service's Endpoints list is empty even though 3 pods matching its
   selector are `Running`. What are the possible causes?
4. Explain the difference between a liveness probe failure and a
   readiness probe failure in terms of what the kubelet vs kube-proxy
   each do about it.
5. Why is base64 in a Secret not "encryption," and what actually protects
   Secret data in a real cluster?
6. What's the actual difference between `ClusterIP`, `NodePort`, and
   `LoadBalancer`, in terms of what layer solves the exposure problem?
7. Why does a StatefulSet need a headless Service, and what breaks
   without it?
8. What's the provision-vs-configure boundary between Terraform and
   Ansible, and why does mixing them up (e.g., using Terraform
   `remote-exec` for configuration) cause problems?
9. Explain PV/PVC/StorageClass as if the interviewer has never heard of
   Kubernetes storage.
10. What is the operator pattern, and name a concrete example you've
    personally run.

## Live debugging scenario (instructor breaks ONE of the below on a fresh kind
cluster with the project's Helm chart installed; ~45 min, narrated)
- Option A: Scale the app's ConfigMap to reference a key that no longer
  exists, breaking new rollouts while old pods keep serving.
- Option B: Apply a NetworkPolicy that inadvertently blocks the app from
  reaching its database Service.
- Option C: Corrupt the CronJob's schedule syntax so reports silently stop
  running (no crash, just absence — the hardest kind of bug to notice).
- Option D: Set a memory limit too low on the db component, causing
  intermittent OOMKills under light load.
- Whichever is chosen: the learner must find and fix it live using only
  `kubectl`/`helm` commands, narrating the Day 73 funnel the whole way,
  with the instructor interjecting follow-up "why" questions at each step
  (interview-realism, not just lab-completion).

## Definition of done
All 10 architecture questions attempted out loud, with the interviewer
noting depth (not just correctness) · the live debugging scenario
diagnosed and fixed using the Day 73 funnel, narrated throughout · a
written gap list produced during the session, not reconstructed afterward.
