# Day 171 — Capstone Build 3: k8s/Helm Deploy + GitOps

Phase 6 · Capstone build sprint, day 3 of 7.

---

## Recall warm-up (5 min)
1. What five stages made up yesterday's hardened CI pipeline?
2. How did you prove the scan step actually catches vulnerabilities?
3. What's still a placeholder from Day 170, if anything?

## Objectives (today)
1. Package the capstone service(s) as a Helm chart (Day 74–75 muscle).
2. Bring the deployment under ArgoCD management with a real sync policy
   (Day 132–134 muscle) — no manual `kubectl apply` from here on.

## Deliverables
- A Helm chart (`values.yaml`, templates, helpers) that deploys the capstone
  service with correct probes, resource requests/limits, and a Service/Ingress
  entry consistent with the rest of the platform.
- An ArgoCD `Application` manifest pointing at the chart, with an automated sync
  policy (self-heal + prune) — matching the main project's D134 configuration.
- A demonstrated rollback: a deliberate bad change synced and then rolled back via
  ArgoCD, not by hand.
- CI updated so a merge to main triggers the GitOps flow (image build/push →
  manifest/values bump → ArgoCD syncs) rather than deploying directly from CI.

## Suggested time-box (3h)
- **0:00–0:20** — Recap the app-of-apps pattern and sync-policy options (auto-prune,
  self-heal) from Day 132/134 before writing the manifest.
- **0:20–1:30** — Write and lint the Helm chart; `helm template`/`helm lint` before
  ever touching the live cluster; deploy manually once to sanity-check, then tear
  down and let ArgoCD own it from here.
- **1:30–2:10** — Wire the ArgoCD `Application`, confirm auto-sync from a git push.
- **2:10–2:40** — Break something on purpose (bad image tag or bad config value),
  sync it, observe the failure, then roll back via ArgoCD and confirm recovery.
- **2:40–3:00** — Checkpoint review.

## Checkpoint (trainer confirms before Day 172 starts)
- Service is live on the cluster, deployed and owned entirely by ArgoCD (no
  drift when `argocd app diff` is run).
- A real rollback was demonstrated, not just described.
- CI no longer runs `kubectl apply`/`helm upgrade` directly — GitOps owns delivery.

## Trainer-review questions
1. What would `argocd app diff` show if someone `kubectl edit`-ed this deployment
   by hand right now, and what would auto-heal do about it?
2. Walk through exactly what happened during the rollback drill, step by step.
3. Why does CI stop at "update the manifest/image tag" instead of deploying directly?
4. What's this service's current resource request/limit, and how did you pick it?

## Definition of done
Helm chart authored and lint-clean, ArgoCD `Application` with auto-sync/self-heal
live, a real break-and-rollback drill completed, CI updated to the GitOps handoff
pattern, checkpoint confirmed.
