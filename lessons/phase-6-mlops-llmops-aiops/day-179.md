# Day 179 — Facilitator Script: Final Mock-Interview Marathon

Phase 6 · follows Day 178 (job sprint). Format: **facilitator script** — a
structured interview simulation, not a lecture.

---

## Session objective
Run the fresher through back-to-back mock interviews (technical, HR/behavioral,
salary basics) covering the full 180-day span, surfacing any remaining gaps
before real interviews start landing from Day 178's applications.

## Materials needed before the session
- The gap list from Day 149 (Phase 5 mock interviews) — check whether those gaps
  were actually closed.
- A question bank spanning all six phases (facilitator prepares in advance):
  Linux/networking troubleshooting (P1), Git/CI/Docker debugging (P2), Kubernetes
  architecture + live debugging (P3), cloud/FinOps scenarios (P4), SRE/SLO math +
  incident response (P5), and one ML/AIOps conceptual question (P6) — reflecting
  the exposure-level framing (interviewers may ask "have you touched ML," not
  expect ML depth).
- A quiet space and a timer — mock interviews only work if they feel real.

## Session outline (3h, time-blocked)

### Block 1 — Technical round (75 min)
- 45 min live-coding/live-debugging scenario on the capstone or a fresh problem
  (e.g., "this pod is crash-looping, find out why" using real `kubectl`/`journalctl`
  commands, timed) — mirrors the CKA-drill format from Day 85–86.
- 30 min whiteboard/verbal system-design question: "design monitoring for a new
  service" or "walk me through your capstone's architecture" — facilitator plays
  a skeptical interviewer, pushes on tradeoffs ("why Helm and not raw manifests,"
  "why this SLO and not a tighter one").
- Facilitator note: do not give answers during the round — hold all feedback for
  the debrief.

### Block 2 — HR/behavioral round (45 min)
- Standard behavioral questions: "tell me about a time you debugged something
  hard," "tell me about a mistake and what you learned," "why DevOps/SRE."
- STAR format coaching (Situation, Task, Action, Result) — use real moments from
  the 180 days (the D145 game day, the D166 anomaly-alert drill, a real chaos-test
  finding from D176) as the story material, not invented anecdotes.
- Practice explaining the ML/AIOps component's exposure-level framing honestly:
  "I built one real ML component and know how the surrounding ecosystem works,
  but my depth is DevOps/SRE" — an honest, confident answer beats overclaiming.

### Block 3 — Salary/negotiation basics (30 min)
- Cover: researching a fair range for the role/location/experience level before
  any offer conversation, never naming a number first if avoidable, how to respond
  to "what are your salary expectations" without under- or over-anchoring, and
  that a written offer is always worth a day to review before accepting.
- Role-play one negotiation exchange.

### Block 4 — Debrief (30 min)
- Go round by round: what landed well, what didn't, and why — be specific, not
  just "good job."
- Update the gap list from Day 149: which gaps closed, which remain, any new ones
  found today.
- Feed remaining gaps into Day 180's "what to learn next" map.

## Facilitator checklist (confirm before ending session)
- [ ] Technical round covered at least 4 of the 6 phases.
- [ ] Behavioral answers use real, specific stories (not invented/generic ones).
- [ ] Salary-basics role-play completed.
- [ ] Updated gap list produced for Day 180.

## Day 180 preview
Graduation: capstone presentation to a wider audience, full retrospective, the
90-day job-hunt plan, and the "what to learn next" map.
