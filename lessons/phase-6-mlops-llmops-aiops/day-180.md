# Day 180 — Facilitator Script: Graduation, Retrospective, 90-Day Job-Hunt Plan

Phase 6 · follows Day 179 (final mock-interview marathon). Format: **facilitator
script** — the closing session of the 180-day program.

---

## Session objective
Close the program with a real capstone presentation, an honest full-program
retrospective, a concrete 90-day job-hunt plan, and a "what to learn next" map so
learning continues past Day 180 with intention instead of drift.

## Materials needed before the session
- The capstone repo, README, diagram, and demo video (Day 177).
- The updated gap list from Day 179.
- The original curriculum blueprint and this program's decisions log (README) for
  the retrospective discussion.
- Anyone else invited to watch the presentation (instructor's choice — a wider
  audience than just the two of them, if desired, raises the stakes usefully).

## Session outline (3h, time-blocked)

### Block 1 — Capstone presentation (45 min)
- Fresher presents the capstone live (15–20 min): problem, architecture, the one
  ML/AIOps component, the chaos/load-test findings, what he'd do differently.
- Q&A (20–25 min) from whoever's watching — treat it like a real interview
  panel, not a formality.

### Block 2 — Full-program retrospective (45 min)
- Structured retro questions, discussed out loud and captured in notes:
  - What phase felt the most valuable, and why?
  - What phase felt rushed or shallow — where would more time have helped most?
  - Which single skill do you feel most confident walking into a job with?
  - Which single skill do you feel least confident in, despite having covered it?
  - Did the review-day cadence (every 7th day) actually help retention — would
    you change the spacing?
- Facilitator also reflects on the teaching side (per the program's own stated
  motivations: market relevance, giving back, personal growth) — capture this too,
  it's part of the record.

### Block 3 — 90-day job-hunt plan (45 min)
- Build a concrete, dated plan (use absolute dates, not "week 1/week 2") covering:
  application cadence (how many/week sustained past Day 178's initial 20),
  a recurring interview-prep rhythm (reuse Day 179's mock format, e.g., biweekly),
  a plan for staying technically current (what to read/build in the gaps between
  interviews), and a decision point/checkpoint date to reassess strategy if no
  offers have landed.
- Write this into a dated document the fresher keeps and owns (e.g.,
  `job-hunt-plan-90day.md` in his own space, or a doc in this project's repo if he
  wants it public).

### Block 4 — What-to-learn-next map (45 min)
- Using Day 179's gap list plus the retrospective's "least confident" answer,
  build a short prioritized list of 3–5 topics to keep sharpening post-graduation
  (e.g., deeper Kubernetes internals, a second cloud, more SRE incident practice) —
  not a new 180-day plan, just a living list he can work from independently.
- Point him at where to keep learning: official docs habits already built
  (`man`, `--help`, vendor docs-first instinct from Day 2 onward), the NotebookLM
  notebooks as a standing study resource, and the public GitHub/YouTube presence
  as ongoing proof-of-work.

## Facilitator checklist (confirm before ending session)
- [ ] Capstone presented and Q&A completed.
- [ ] Retrospective captured in writing (both fresher's and facilitator's reflections).
- [ ] 90-day job-hunt plan written with absolute dates and a reassessment checkpoint.
- [ ] What-to-learn-next list produced (3–5 items, prioritized).

## Program closing note
This is the last `day-NNN.md` in the 180-day curriculum. Ongoing progress (job
outcomes, further learning) belongs in the project's own tracking going forward,
not in new day files — the curriculum's job was to get here.
